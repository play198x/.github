# Support

Questions and ideas are welcome.

- **Bugs and format questions** — open an issue on the relevant repo in the
  [play198x](https://github.com/play198x) org. If a file plays wrongly, bring the
  file.
- **Requesting a format** — say what you want to play and where the files come
  from. Formats arrive as their decoders do.
- **Status** — the core decodes images and ProTracker modules today; chip-backed
  audio waits on published Emu198x chip crates. See the org profile page.
- **Security** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); please report privately.

It's a small project, so replies may take a little while. Thanks for your
patience.
